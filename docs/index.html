How the automatic scan works

A free scheduled scanner checks ranked Google Play game collections every 6 hours and updates this dashboard's game index. Selecting a date filters the saved index instantly by the app's initial released date.

Google Play provides no complete public “all games released on this date” directory. Results are accurate for discovered games, but very small or unranked launches can be missed.
